CheckUp Plus

Yes (Delta Dental PPO / Premier / Out-of-Network). CheckUp Plus allows enrollees to get diagnostic and preventive dental services without those costs getting applied to the individual annual maximum – leaving more flexibility for restorative care that might be needed later.

Evidence-Based Integrated Care Plan (EBICP)

Yes (Delta Dental PPO / Premier / Out-of-Network). Evidence-Based Integrated Care Plan (EBICP) provides additional cleaning(s) and/or fluoride treatments to individuals with specific medical conditions that have oral implications.
