Tech credits, in plain terms

Many carriers offer credits that offset the cost of benefits communication and technology. They are usually tied to a client's carrier relationship and plan year, and the rules vary by carrier, so always confirm eligibility and paperwork with your carrier representative.

Technology credits

Support for platforms that improve how benefits information is delivered, understood and tracked.

Enrollment credits

Support for communication and education that drives open enrollment participation.

Wellness credits

Support for content that promotes wellness programs and helps employees use them.

Why it matters for brokers: funding the work with carrier credits can mean better client communication with little or no added cost to the employer, and a stronger story at renewal.

Questions

Will my carrier definitely cover this?

No. Credit programs, amounts and eligibility differ by carrier and client. The letter opens the conversation, and your carrier decides.

Can I use the credits for more than open enrollment?

Often, yes. Technology and wellness credits can support year-round education, onboarding and recruiting content. Confirm with your carrier.

What do I need from Relayto AI?

Usually a proposal or invoice describing the scope. Contact us and we will provide one.

Can I run this for several clients?

Yes. Each client can have its own branded hubs and content.
