Case study · Investor relations

investors.adnoc.ae: the Group's investor story, live and on brand

Built on RELAYTO with ADNOC Group Investor Relations. The first draft took 2 days, and the IR team has edited the site ever since.

1 · Before the project

Investor content lived on the corporate site, with no live data and no analytics

  • Corporate release cycle

    Investor pages were a section of adnoc.ae, so every update waited for the next release.

  • No Euroland integration

    Share data for the six listed companies sat on six separate sites.

  • No engagement data

    Results and presentations were static PDFs that couldn't be measured.

  • No visibility

    No view of how investors actually used the content.

  • High production cost

    Every change needed developer time and resources.

  • Off brand

    The pages didn't follow ADNOC's new design language.

2 · The problem

The data was outdated, and no one could see what investors cared about

  • Outdated data

    Prices, returns and filings on the page lagged behind the market.

  • No investor insight

    No visibility into what investors actually read and care about.

  • Split equity story

    The story was spread across press releases and subsidiary pages.

  • Compliance friction

    Debt content needed a residency check and a disclaimer before access.

  • Invisible to AI

    AI crawlers were blocked, so search and AI engines couldn't read the pages.

  • Weak first impression

    A Group raising capital worldwide needed an investor site to match.

3 · The solution

A no-code RELAYTO hub with Euroland data built in

  • Engagement

    Up to 8x

    more time spent than on the PDFs and static pages it replaces.

  • Incoming traffic

    Up to 3x

    more search and AI referrals to one dedicated investor domain.

  • SEO & AI

    4x

    more SEO and AI readability across the investor site.
